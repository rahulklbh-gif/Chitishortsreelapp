# 📱 Chiti Shorts Reel - Mobile Installation Guide

Welcome! Follow these simple steps to install **Chiti Shorts Reel** on your phone.

---

## 🍎 **For iPhone/iPad (iOS/iPadOS)**

### Method 1: Using Safari (Recommended)

1. **Open Safari browser** on your iPhone/iPad
2. **Navigate to your app URL** (the Figma Make deployment URL)
3. **Tap the Share button** (the square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen
4. **Scroll down** and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
5. **Edit the name** if you want (default is "Chiti Shorts")
6. **Tap "Add"** in the top right corner
7. **Done!** The app icon will appear on your home screen

### Features on iOS:
- ✅ Full-screen experience (no browser bars)
- ✅ App icon on home screen
- ✅ Works like a native app
- ✅ Offline support (cached content)

### Important Notes for iOS:
- Must use **Safari** browser (won't work in Chrome/Firefox on iOS)
- The app will remember your position when you close and reopen it
- Videos may need WiFi for initial loading to save data

---

## 🤖 **For Android Phones**

### Method 1: Chrome Browser (Easiest)

1. **Open Chrome browser** on your Android phone
2. **Navigate to your app URL**
3. **Wait for the install prompt** to appear (banner at bottom)
   - If prompt appears, tap **"Install"** or **"Add to Home screen"**
4. **If no prompt appears:**
   - Tap the **three dots menu** (⋮) in the top right
   - Select **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**
5. **Tap "Install"** in the confirmation dialog
6. **Done!** The app will be added to your app drawer and home screen

### Method 2: Other Browsers (Firefox, Edge, Samsung Internet)

1. **Open your browser** and go to the app URL
2. **Tap the menu button** (usually three dots or lines)
3. **Look for:**
   - "Add to Home screen"
   - "Install"
   - "Add to phone"
4. **Follow the prompts** to install
5. **Done!**

### Features on Android:
- ✅ Full-screen app experience
- ✅ App icon in launcher
- ✅ Background notifications (if enabled)
- ✅ Offline support
- ✅ App appears in recent apps switcher

## 🔧 **Troubleshooting**

### "Add to Home Screen" option not showing?
- **iOS**: Make sure you're using Safari, not another browser
- **Android**: Try using Chrome browser instead
- Clear browser cache and reload the page

### App not working offline?
- The first time you open the app, it needs internet to load
- After initial load, basic navigation works offline
- Videos require internet to stream

### App icon not appearing?
- Wait a few seconds after installation
- Check your home screen and app drawer
- Try restarting your phone

### Videos not playing?
- Check your internet connection
- Allow autoplay in browser settings
- Tap on video to unmute/play

### Install prompt keeps appearing?
- Dismiss it using the X button
- The app will remember your choice
- Clear app data to see prompt again

If you have issues installing:
