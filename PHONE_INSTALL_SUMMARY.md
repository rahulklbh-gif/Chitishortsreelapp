## 📲 Step 2: Install Based on Your Device

### 🍎 **iPhone/iPad Users** (iOS 11.3+)

1. **Open Safari** (must be Safari, not Chrome!)
2. **Paste your app URL** in the address bar
3. **Tap the Share button** at the bottom (square with arrow ↑)
4. **Scroll down** in the menu
5. **Tap "Add to Home Screen"**
6. **Edit name** (optional): "Chiti Shorts"
7. **Tap "Add"** in top right

**✨ Done!** Find the app icon on your home screen.

### 🤖 **Android Users** (Android 5.0+)

#### Method A: Automatic Prompt (Easiest)
1. **Open Chrome** browser
2. **Visit your app URL**
3. **Wait 3 seconds** - an install banner appears at bottom
4. **Tap "Install"** or "Add Chiti Shorts"
5. **Done!**

#### Method B: Manual Install
1. **Open Chrome** browser
2. **Visit your app URL**
3. **Tap the 3 dots** (⋮) in top right corner
4. **Look for:**
   - "Install app" OR
   - "Add to Home screen"
5. **Tap it**
6. **Confirm** by tapping "Install" or "Add"

**✨ Done!** Find the app in your app drawer and home screen.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Problem: "Add to Home Screen" missing on iPhone
**Solution:** You MUST use Safari browser. Chrome/Firefox won't work on iOS.

### Problem: Install prompt doesn't appear on Android
**Solution:** 
- Use Chrome browser
- Manually use menu (⋮) → "Install app"
- Clear browser cache and try again

### Problem: Videos not playing
**Solution:**
- Check internet connection
- Tap video to play/unmute
- Allow autoplay in browser settings

### Problem: App icon not showing
**Solution:**
- Wait 10-30 seconds
- Check app drawer (Android)
- Restart phone

### Problem: App keeps opening in browser
**Solution:**
- Delete bookmark if you bookmarked it
- Reinstall using "Add to Home Screen"
- Make sure you tapped "Install" not "Bookmark"
